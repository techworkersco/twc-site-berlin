We meet at **[Südblock Cafe](https://maps.app.goo.gl/X5UYJN4ApijevwGf7)** (near Kottbusser Tor), a non-smoking queer friendly space with snacks, drinks and multiple tables to socialise. We typically meet from 19:00-21:00. You can stay longer of course!


The social meetup is open for new and regular attendees alike. Make friends, ask questions and meet with people from our internal Telegram group chat.

## Venue

Südblock is wheelchair-accessible (including bathroom) and non-smoking. Dogs are welcome!
