For experienced and new works council members alike. We pick one specific topic each month: This month SumUp Works Council will present what co-determination do you have regarding **selection guidelines** for hiring, transferring or firing employees in **BetrVG [§95](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/englisch_betrvg/englisch_betrvg.html#p0559)**? We will then all share best practices and experiences with each other. **[Online Registration is mandatory](https://us02web.zoom.us/meeting/register/4zLXGLPgTrO3UjxpVZEcwg)**.

Whether you formed a Works Council one week ago or 10 years ago, we want you here! We are here to support each other with advice, questions and collaboration! If there is a topic you are interested in, offer to moderate the following Works Council round-table!
