Ver.di trade union and Tech Workers Coalition are organising a joint conference open to all ver.di members. 

This single-track conference will explore the intersections of tech-driven fascism, antifascist economics, role of generative AI and the role of organized labor. 

We are still confirming speakers and aim to include a mix of technical-experts, academic researchers and trade-union activists organizing against right-wing fascism in the heart of Germany.
