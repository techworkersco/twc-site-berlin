We are movements and anti-capitalist activists who practice various forms of resistance against big tech. We want to bring together interested people, activists and movements, network and connect local struggles. We want to deepen our understanding of the problem, exchange ideas about necessary forms of action, develop strategies together and advance the resistance.

Tech Workers Coalition Berlin is involved particularly with the labor organising track together with the eight other tracks:

* Urban policy 
* Water and raw materials 
* Labour 
* Climate and energy 
* Militarisation 
* Fascism 
* Feminism 
* Futures 

Cables of Resistance is organised by:

* Berlin Tech Workers Coalition
* [Berlin Vs Amazon](https://berlinvsamazon.noblogs.org/)
* [Tesla den Hahn Abdrehen](https://www.instagram.com/tesla_den_hahn_abdrehen/)
* [Lause Berlin](https://lause.berlin/de)
