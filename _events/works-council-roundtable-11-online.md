For experienced and new works council members alike. We pick one specific topic each month (**Objections to employment dismissals** this month **[§102 hearings](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/englisch_betrvg/englisch_betrvg.html#p0597)**) and after a short intro, share best practices and experiences with each other. **[Online Registration is mandatory](https://us02web.zoom.us/meeting/register/7yi_q2gSQoKW82jKxpxGOQ#/registration)**.

Whether you formed a Works Council one week ago or 10 years ago, we want you here! We are here to support each other with advice, questions and collaboration! If there is a topic you are interested in, offer to moderate the following Works Council round-table!
